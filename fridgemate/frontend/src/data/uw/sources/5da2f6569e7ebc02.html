About:

We accept Dining Account, Husky Card Account, and most major credit/debit cards.

Note: All campus dining locations are open to the UW community.

Located at the heart of four HFS residence halls on scenic North Campus, Center Table offers nine innovative food stations.

Dawg House Pizza: Grab a slice or a whole pie from our pizza counter that features a variety of toppings and flavors. Also try our salad, garlic knots, baked pasta, cannoli and more!

Global: Weekly journeys into regional cuisines, showcasing flavors, ingredients and cooking techniques from around the globe.

Market Deli: Breakfast, hot and cold made-to-order sandwiches, and a rotating selection of side dishes, including soups and salads.

Noodle: Build-your-own pasta bar with weekly rotating specials.

Plate: Kick-start your day with a variety of breakfast favorites and daily sweet, savory, and scramble rotating specials. Fuel your afternoon and evening with daily rotating specials for lunch and dinner.

Quench: A spot for all your beverage needs, with coffee, tea, milkshakes, soft-serve ice cream, and specialty drinks, as well as cookies and other snacks.

Salad Bar: Yogurt and fruit breakfast bar and salad bar for lunch and dinner.

Seared: Our signature grill, featuring the classic Dub burger, chicken sandwiches, tenders, milkshakes, fries, and rotating special sandwiches. Customize your burger with a choice of plant-based, turkey or beef patties.

Select: A dedicated Big 9 and gluten-free platform on North Campus. While Select is within a shared dining hall, it has its own designated kitchen and equipment and is overseen by staff with extra training around cross-contact prevention.
