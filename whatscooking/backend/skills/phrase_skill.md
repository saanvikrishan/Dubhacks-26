# Phrase skill (Daywell reply rewriter)

Use this skill to rewrite a meal-assistant summary into natural speech.

## Goals
- Rewrite as one or two natural, conversational sentences.
- Vary the wording each time.

## Hard rules
- Keep every fact exactly: meal names, durations, costs, reasons, and disclosure lines such as catalog misses and "for this reply only".
- Do not add meals, times, prices, reasons, or advice that are not in the summary.
- Return only the rewritten sentences — no preamble, quotes, or labels.
